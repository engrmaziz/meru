# Phase 9 — Jobs, Invoices & Vault Writeback

**Codename:** Stamp  
**Goal:** Check-in → work → bill → owner confirm → certified history in Meru vault.  
**Depends on:** Phase 8  
**Unlocks:** Phase 10 marketplace-ready  

---

## Scope
1. Job lifecycle: confirmed → checked_in → in_progress → extras → billed → closed
2. Staff check-in + shared history viewer (scope-enforced)
3. Extra-work approval flow (owner approve/deny)
4. Invoice builder (labor/parts/fees) + PDF to R2
5. Owner invoice review → confirm → **certified service_record writeback**
6. Dispute basic
7. Reviews (job-gated) + workshop rating
8. Workshop staff **web portal MVP** (Next.js): calendar, job, invoice (DEC-014)
9. Gamify: vault “certified stamp” animation on writeback
10. Strix pass #1 on bookings/invoices/shares (staging)

## Out of scope
In-app payments, featured listings monetization, multi-branch chains

## Exit criteria
- [ ] Confirm writes taxonomy-mapped service into timeline
- [ ] No writeback without confirm
- [ ] Share expiry denies staff
- [ ] Review requires completed job
- [ ] BUILD-LOG + Strix notes

This phase completes the owner promise: “service saves itself into my car’s life.”
