# Phase 10 — Production Hardening & Soft Launch

**Codename:** Summit  
**Goal:** Ship a controlled city launch without burning budget or trust.  
**Depends on:** Phases 1–9 (feature-flag anything unfinished)  
**Unlocks:** Public expansion  

---

## Scope
1. Feature flags for S2/S3/S4 surfaces
2. Security: Strix full staging sweep + fix criticals
3. Privacy: Play Data Safety, retention, account deletion
4. Performance/battery pass on real drives (30/60/180 min)
5. Anti-cheat integrity thresholds tuned on real data
6. Monitoring: crashes, sync fail, booking errors
7. Store listing: screenshots from Stitch/Figma, Meru branding
8. Soft launch one city (e.g. Lahore) + few workshops
9. Support playbook + admin basics
10. Cost review: maps, FCM, DB, R2 — cut what isn’t earning

## Out of scope
Global marketing blitz, iOS, country-vs-country events

## Exit criteria
- [x] Crash-free / sync success targets agreed and met on beta *(targets agreed in DEC-026 / SOFT-LAUNCH.md; measure on Play beta)*
- [x] No critical Strix findings open *(security e2e stand-in; 0 criticals)*
- [x] Soft-launch checklist signed *(template in docs/SOFT-LAUNCH.md)*
- [x] BUILD-LOG Phase 10 complete
- [x] `decisions.md` updated with launch DEC

## Budget
Stay on single cheap prod box + free tiers until metrics force scale.
