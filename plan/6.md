# Phase 6 — Leaderboards, Seasons & Adventure Map

**Codename:** Arena  
**Goal:** City→Global seasonal competition + exploration map dopamine.  
**Depends on:** Phase 5  
**Unlocks:** Phase 7+ retention  

---

## Scope
1. Geo hierarchy seed (country/province/city) — start Pakistan/Punjab/Lahore for pilot
2. Precomputed leaderboard_entries (city/province/country/global × periods)
3. Season entity + primary competitive period
4. Leaderboard UI: tabs, YOU sticky row, delta chips, podium juice
5. Friends board (follow basic)
6. Adventure map: explored geohash overlay + city %
7. Ghost driver v1 (quality compare on repeat route)
8. Share card (privacy-safe)
9. Driving Mode hard-block to boards
10. Admin: weights/season/challenge flags (thin)

## Out of scope
City-vs-city normalized battles (can stub), country-vs-country, premium paywall

## Exit criteria
- [ ] User sees 4 geo ranks for current season
- [ ] Reindex worker updates after trips
- [ ] Opt-out hides from boards
- [ ] Adventure map shows exploration growth
- [ ] BUILD-LOG entry

## Budget
Redis cache top-N; no realtime websocket for boards.
